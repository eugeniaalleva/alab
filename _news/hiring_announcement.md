# Open Positions – Applied Computational Women's Health Research Lab at Mount Sinai

We’re building a new lab at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, backed by the Wellcome Leap *Missed Vital Sign* program.  
Our mission: to close critical gaps in women’s health by applying cutting-edge AI to one of the most overlooked challenges in medicine — heavy menstrual bleeding and iron deficiency.  

As a founding team member, you’ll have access to unique resources including:  
- **Sinai Million** (genome-linked EHR biobank)  
- **AIRMS** (AI-Ready Mount Sinai infrastructure)  
- **Minerva HPC cluster**  
- **eHive** (digital study platform)  
- **Global partnerships** with the Hasso Plattner Institute in Germany  

We’re hiring across multiple roles — from postdocs to research staff — to build an interdisciplinary group at the intersection of AI, medicine, and real-world data.  

---

## Postdoctoral Fellow – Computer Vision & Multimodal AI
We’re seeking a postdoc with expertise in **computer vision and multimodal AI** to work across radiology, pathology, genomics, and EHR data. This is an opportunity to help develop translational tools with real clinical impact, collaborating directly with clinicians and AI scientists. You’ll join as a founding member of a new group that values openness, collaboration, and impact, with resources few labs can offer and opportunities for international collaboration.

---

## Postdoctoral Fellow – Time Series & Wearable Data
We’re seeking a postdoc with expertise in **time-series modeling, wearable data, or mobile health**. You’ll develop methods that make sense of high-frequency, messy, real-world signals and work with clinicians to ensure these models translate into meaningful insights for patients. You’ll join a collaborative environment with access to genome-linked EHR data, digital health platforms, and global partnerships.

---

## Postdoctoral Fellow – AI for CVD and Cognitive Decline
We’re seeking a postdoc to join the *DORIAN GRAY* project, focusing on **predictive modeling for cardiovascular disease (CVD) and mild cognitive impairment (MCI)**. You’ll apply large language models to extract cognitive measures from clinical notes and integrate EHR and biobank data to build multimodal risk models. This role offers the opportunity to advance methods that bridge genomics, informatics, and patient care while collaborating with a diverse team of clinical and data science experts.

---

## Machine Learning Scientist I – Translational AI
We’re looking for a **Machine Learning Scientist I** to serve as the technical backbone of the lab — building, training, and optimizing models across EHR, imaging, wearable, and genomic data. You’ll run large-scale experiments on Minerva HPC, support distributed training, and contribute to pipelines that enable true translation of AI methods into the clinic. This role is ideal for an applied researcher or engineer who thrives on HPC-scale challenges and wants their work to directly shape women’s health.

---

## Associate Researcher I – NLP
We’re seeking an **Associate Researcher I with NLP expertise** to help build the largest curated dataset of menstrual disorders from EHR data. You’ll work hands-on with NLP pipelines, coding in Python, and refining models for clinical text. Hugging Face experience or prior EHR work is a plus, but not required. This role is ideal for someone motivated to deepen their expertise in NLP and contribute directly to women’s health research.

---

## Research Coordinator (with Project Management focus)
We’re looking for a **Research Coordinator / Project Manager** to keep our studies running smoothly, coordinate across clinical and technical teams, and help deliver on ambitious milestones. You’ll be part of the core team shaping lab processes, supporting clinicians and AI scientists, and ensuring that projects stay on track in this mission-driven, interdisciplinary program.
