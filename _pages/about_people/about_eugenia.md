Dr. Eugenia Alleva is an Assistant Professor at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, where she leads the Applied Computational Women’s Health Research Lab (A-Lab). She also serves as Associate Scientific Director of AI-Ready Mount Sinai (AIRMS), the institution’s flagship platform for large-scale data integration and AI-driven health innovation. Trained as both a physician (M.D., Università Vita-Salute San Raffaele, summa cum laude) and digital health scientist (M.Sc., Hasso Plattner Institute, Germany), she bridges gynecology and computational sciences to advance women’s health.

Her research applies machine learning and natural language processing to gynecological and obstetrical conditions, including endometriosis, dysmenorrhea, and heavy menstrual bleeding (HMB). In 2025, she received a Wellcome Leap Missed Vital Sign grant to develop AI/ML tools for early detection and treatment of HMB, a condition affecting over 30% of reproductive-age women worldwide.
