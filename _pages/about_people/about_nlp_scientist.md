We’re seeking an **Associate Researcher I with a focus on natural language processing (NLP)** to join our new lab at Mount Sinai, launched with support from the Wellcome Leap Missed Vital Sign program. Our mission is to close critical gaps in women’s health by applying AI to one of the most neglected challenges: heavy menstrual bleeding and iron deficiency.

In this role, you will contribute to building the **largest curated dataset of menstrual disorders from electronic health records**. You’ll work hands-on with NLP pipelines, coding primarily in Python, and supporting the development and refinement of models for clinical text. Familiarity with Hugging Face tools or prior experience with EHR data is a plus, but not required.

This is a **junior position ideal for someone who enjoys coding**, wants to deepen their expertise in NLP, and is motivated to see their work make a direct impact on women’s health. You’ll collaborate closely with postdocs, data scientists, and clinicians, and play a key role in creating foundational resources for our lab and the broader research community.
