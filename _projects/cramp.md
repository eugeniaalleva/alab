# What is this study about? 
The CRAMP Study is a fully remote and digital study, which aims at characterizing how stress resilience is associated to menstrual cramps. You will be asked to complete daily surveys on your phone and connect you Apple Watch to our study app, ehive. To measure your resilience, we will also ask you to perform a digital resilience test through our study app. 


# Who can participate?

To be eligible to participate in this study, an individual must meet all of the following criteria: 

- Between the ages of 18 and 40 

- Have regular menstrual cycles every 21 to 40 days 

- Are not currently taking, or have not taken, hormonal contraceptives (e.g. the pill) within the past three months 

- Are not pregnant or lactating 

- Have not had surgery in the last 3 months 

- Are not currently ill 

- Own an Apple iPhone 

- Can read  English 

# Is there any compensation?
You may receive **up to $100** for participating in this study, depending on how much of it you complete.
There is no medical benefit to participating, but your involvement will directly contribute to research on a very common condition that affects up to 90% of menstruating people—and remains significantly understudied.

# How can I participate?
Use this [link](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ehive-studies/id1536952138) to download the ehive study app and enroll in the CRAMP Study.
Alternatively, you can search for ehive directly in your app store and download it on your phone.
